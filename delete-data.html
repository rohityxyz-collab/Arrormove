🗑️ How to Request & Erase Your Game Data

HIDE store and developer Rohit Yadav respect your privacy rights. Arrow Move: Escape Puzzle is built as an offline-first game where your progress is stored locally on your device. You have full control to permanently delete your data at any time using the steps below:

1

In-Game Reset (Instant)

Open Arrow Move > Tap Settings ⚙ > Scroll down > Tap RESET ALL GAME PROGRESS. Confirms immediate erasure of levels and coins.

2

Android Settings Wipe

On your phone, go to Settings > Apps > Arrow Move > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. Instantly purges all local SQLite databases.

3

App Uninstallation

Uninstalling Arrow Move from your Android phone immediately and permanently destroys all local save files from your device storage.

4

Manual Email Request

If you have contacted our support team and want any email records deleted, send an email to support@example.com. Handled within 24 hours.

📋 Types of Data Deleted & Retention Policy

  • Data Subject to Deletion: All puzzle stage completion records, coins balance, unlocked skins/avatars, statistics, and sound/vibration preferences.
  • Data Retained: Zero personal data is stored on remote cloud servers. No identity data is kept.
  • Retention Period: Deletion takes effect immediately upon using in-app reset, clearing device storage, or uninstalling.
