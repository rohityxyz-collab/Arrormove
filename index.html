Official Privacy Policy

Last Revised & Certified: September 2026 • Published by HIDE store

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which HIDE store and lead developer Rohit Yadav manage user privacy in Arrow Move: Escape Puzzle (com.arrowpuzzlegame.byrohityadav).

"Our fundamental principle: We do not collect, store, sell, or transfer your personal identity data. Your puzzle journey remains your own."

1. Zero Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Collection

Arrow Move: Escape Puzzle is architected as an offline-first logic puzzle application. We do NOT require, track, or collect:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your real name, email address, physical address, or mobile number.
  • No Sensitive Hardware Access: We never request access to your Contacts, Camera, Microphone, SMS, or precise GPS Location.
  • No Account Mandatory: You do not need to register an account or create login credentials to enjoy the complete game.

2. Strict Prohibition on Third-Party Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or transfer user information to data brokers, analytics marketing firms, or unrelated third parties. All puzzle progress remains strictly confined to your physical hardware.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob 2026 Integration)

To provide free gameplay, the application integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and use non-sensitive, pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) in compliance with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Identifiers are utilized solely for ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, and delivering non-intrusive ads.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized interest-based ads anytime via:
    Android Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset Advertising ID.
  • Review Google's official privacy commitments: Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy & Family Safety (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Move: Escape Puzzle complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. We do not intentionally harvest data from children under 13.

5. Data Retention & Player Deletion Rights

Since your puzzle progress, coins, and records are stored locally via SQLite, you have complete control over your data:

  • In-Game Reset: You can reset all progress, levels, and coins anytime from Settings > Reset All Game Progress.
  • Uninstall Deletion: Uninstalling the application completely erases all local save files from your device immediately.
